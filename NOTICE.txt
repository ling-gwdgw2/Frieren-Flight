2. CLEAN-ROOM ORIGINALITY STATEMENT
-----------------------------------
Zoltraak: Cinematic Edition is authored under All Rights Reserved and built
with original clean-room implementations across all Java code, keyframe camera
rig choreography (CinematicRigController, CameraTrack, CameraTransform,
CameraRigFrame, CameraObstacleSolver), Blaze3D/JOML procedural geometry,
custom Qual boss model/animations/textures, synthesized audio, and all 26
GLSL 150 core shaders (covering all 8 registered spells):
  - Early pre-1.0.4 spell prototypes were permanently replaced in Version 1.0.4.
  - gargantua.fsh and its 3 post-processing bloom passes (gargantua_bloom_down,
    gargantua_bloom_up, gargantua_composite) are written clean-room around
    inverse-square gravitational geodesic raymarching, lattice 3D value noise &
    FBM accretion disk rendering, blackbody/plasma shading, Karis-weighted HDR
    downsampling, bicubic B-spline upsampling, and ACES filmic tone mapping.
  - Explosion Magic & pyroclastic impact shaders (explosion_dome,
    explosion_dust_wave, explosion_vortex_ring, zoltraak_blast_dome,
    zoltraak_pyroclast) are authored clean-room in GLSL 150 with multi-pass
    additive/translucent volumetric layering.
  - client/cinematic/ was rewritten from scratch around an original keyframe
    timeline and camera obstacle solver architecture.
  - qual_boss (.geo.json, .animation.json, .png, _glowmask.png) is a clean-room
    original creation with zero third-party skins or character models.
